The Challenge

A five-facility radiology group processing thousands of claims monthly couldn't confidently answer: "Are we actually getting paid for everything we billed?"

Payment reconciliation was done manually — a slow, error-prone process leaving significant revenue gaps undetected. Leadership suspected money was slipping through the cracks but had no way to quantify how much or where.

The core problem: With 31,000+ claims per billing cycle, manual reconciliation meant auditors could only spot-check a fraction of records — leaving most discrepancies invisible.

2

The Approach

Automated RCM deployed an AI-powered billing reconciliation analysis across all 31,863 claims — every single record, not a sample.

1

Data Ingestion & Cleaning

Billing records were consolidated, standardized, and deduplicated into a single source of truth.

2

AI-Powered Claim Matching

Every billed claim was matched against payment records using intelligent reconciliation logic.

3

Zero-Pay & Underpayment Analysis

Claims with zero payment were isolated and categorized by reason: denial, no response, payer error, or billing issue.

4

Actionable Report Delivery

Results delivered in a structured report, prioritized by dollar value and recovery likelihood.

The Results

  • Full billing visibility — Leadership gained a complete, reconciled view of all claims and payments for the first time.
  • Prioritized recovery roadmap — The $89K in zero-pay claims was organized by dollar value for immediate follow-up.
  • Process improvement insight — Analysis revealed which payers and claim types had the highest non-payment rates.
  • Repeatable audit framework — A reusable AI audit process now runs on future billing cycles without starting from scratch.
