The Challenge

A five-facility radiology group processing thousands of claims monthly had a problem that's all too common in healthcare: they couldn't confidently answer the question "Are we actually getting paid for everything we billed?"

Their billing data was siloed across multiple systems. Payment reconciliation was done manually — a slow, error-prone process that left significant revenue gaps undetected. Leadership suspected money was slipping through the cracks but had no way to quantify how much or where.

The core problem: With 31,000+ claims per billing cycle, manual reconciliation meant auditors could only spot-check a fraction of records — leaving the majority of potential discrepancies invisible.

2

The Approach

Automated RCM deployed an AI-powered billing reconciliation analysis across all 31,863 claims in the group's dataset. Rather than sampling, the analysis covered every single record — something no manual process could accomplish in a reasonable timeframe.

1

Data Ingestion & Cleaning

Billing records from all five facilities were consolidated, standardized, and deduplicated to create a single source of truth.

2

AI-Powered Claim Matching

Every billed claim was matched against payment records using intelligent reconciliation logic — catching discrepancies that rule-based systems miss.

3

Zero-Pay & Underpayment Analysis

Claims that received zero payment were isolated and categorized by reason — denial, no response, payer error, or billing issue.

4

Actionable Report Delivery

Results were delivered in a structured Excel report with prioritized action items, organized by dollar value and recovery likelihood.

Key insight: The $89K in zero-pay claims was not due to billing errors — it was due to payer non-response that the manual process had failed to flag for follow-up. With AI, every unpaid claim was surfaced automatically.

4

The Results

Beyond the immediate revenue recovery opportunity, this engagement delivered lasting operational value:

  • Full billing visibility — Leadership now had a complete, reconciled view of all claims and payments for the first time.
  • Prioritized action list — The $89K in zero-pay claims was organized by dollar value, giving the billing team a clear recovery roadmap.
  • Process improvement insight — Analysis revealed which payers and claim types had the highest non-payment rates, enabling targeted follow-up protocols.
  • Repeatable audit framework — The group now has a reusable AI audit process they can run on future billing cycles without starting from scratch.

Is This Happening at Your Practice?

Zero-pay claims and unreconciled billing records are not unique to radiology. This pattern shows up across surgical centers, urgent care groups, and any multi-provider practice relying on manual reconciliation processes.

The question isn't whether revenue is slipping through — it's how much. An AI-powered audit answers that question in days, not months.

  • Do you know your current clean claim rate?
  • Can you see every zero-pay claim from the last 90 days?

If the answer to any of these is "no" or "not easily," there's likely uncollected revenue in your billing data right now.
